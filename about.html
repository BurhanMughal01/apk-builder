Our story

APKForge started in 2024 when our founder Burhan Ahmad Mughal got tired of setting up Android Studio just to package a simple HTML prototype into an APK for a client demo.

The existing tools were either too complex (full Android IDE) or too limited (single-page wrappers). So we built APKForge — a tool that takes any HTML or URL and produces a signed, installable, production-ready APK in under a minute.

Today, over 12,000 developers use APKForge to ship apps for clients, side projects, and internal tools.
