About Us

Built with Passion

A free tool for creators, developers, and dreamers who want to ship real apps

APKForge was born from a simple frustration — converting a website into a real Android app shouldn't require weeks of learning Android Studio, dealing with Java/Kotlin code, or paying hundreds of dollars to developers.

In 2026, I built a lightweight solution using cloud technology and open-source tools. Now anyone — a student, a shopkeeper, a small business owner, or a hobbyist developer — can turn their HTML file, website, or template into a signed, ready-to-install Android APK in under 2 minutes.

No signup. No credit card. No coding. Just upload → customize → download. And it will always be free for personal use.

— Burhan Ahmad Mughal, Founder

Journey

How We Got Here

From a weekend project to a tool used by thousands.

January 2026
The Idea
Burhan Ahmad Mughal built the first prototype using Termux on an Android phone — proving that professional apps could be built without expensive tools.
February 2026
First Build System
Deployed the first cloud-based APK builder using GitHub Actions and Cloudflare Workers — free for everyone.
March 2026
Multi-Mode Support
Added URL to APK and 10 ready-made templates, expanding possibilities from just HTML files to full websites.
Today
1,200+ Apps Built
Thousands of developers, students, and businesses use APKForge every month. And we're just getting started.
