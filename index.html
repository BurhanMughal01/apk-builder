FAQ

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know.

Is APKForge really free?
Yes, 100% free for personal use. Unlimited builds, no credit card, no hidden fees. Business plans available for commercial features.
How long does it take to build?
Typically 60-90 seconds. Our cloud build pipeline is optimized for speed.
Will my app work offline?
Yes! Your HTML and all assets are bundled inside the APK. It works completely offline.
What about JavaScript support?
Full JavaScript support with localStorage, DOM manipulation, and Web APIs. Anything that works in Chrome works in your app.
Is the APK signed?
Yes, every APK is signed with a valid keystore (v1 + v2 signing). Ready to install on any Android device.
Can I update my app later?
Absolutely. Just rebuild with new files and share the updated APK. Users can install the update over the previous version.
What content is not allowed?
We only support Halal, ethical apps. No gambling, adult content, dating, music, or interest-based finance.
