Zakat Guide

What is Zakat?

Zakat is one of the five pillars of Islam. It's an obligatory charity of 2.5% on your accumulated wealth held for one lunar year.

Who must pay?

Every adult Muslim who owns wealth equal to or above the Nisab threshold for one full lunar year must pay Zakat.

Nisab Threshold

Nisab is the minimum wealth. It equals 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver. Current Nisab is calculated based on silver rate.

What is Zakatable?

Cash, bank savings, gold, silver, business inventory, investments, and property held for resale. Your primary home and personal items are exempt.

How to Calculate

Total all zakatable assets, subtract immediate liabilities, and if the result exceeds Nisab, pay 2.5% of it as Zakat.

Who receives it?

The poor, needy, those in debt, travelers in need, and other categories mentioned in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60).
