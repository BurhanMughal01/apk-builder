Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Hamara Wada

Hum aap ka data nahi bechte. Hum koi ads nahi dikhate. Hum koi tracking pixels nahi lagate. Ye hamara aqeedah hai.

2. Kya Data Collect Karte Hain

Account banane pe: Email address, password (hashed), naam (optional).

Build karne pe: App name, package name, HTML/URL content, splash config — sirf APK banane ke liye.

Automatically: IP address (rate limiting ke liye), basic error logs.

3. Data Kahan Store Hota Hai

Supabase (US/EU servers) mein encrypted. Cloudflare Worker se process hota hai. Ye trusted infrastructure hai.

4. Data Kaun Dekh Sakta Hai

Sirf aap aur platform admin (bug fix ke liye). Kisi third party ko nahi dete. Kisi advertiser ko nahi dete.

5. Aap Ke Haq

Apna data delete kar sakte hain — email karein contact page se. Account delete pe sab data hat jata hai.

6. Cookies

Sirf session aur theme preference ke liye. Koi advertising cookie nahi.

7. Contact

Sawal? Contact page se poochein.
